#### 1. Second-order MoE expert pruning (HOPE)

HOPE replaces first-order expert-importance pruning criteria (e.g., REAP) with a second-order objective that accounts for cooperative interactions between experts when deciding which to remove. It costs additional calibration computation to estimate interaction terms, requires access to a representative calibration set, and does not reduce the non-expert portion of the model, so memory savings are limited to the pruned expert parameters. It can fail if the calibration data does not match the production task distribution, since the cooperative structure it preserves is estimated from that data, and the demonstrated gains were measured on frontier models (up to 122B parameters) that exceed the reader's hardware even after pruning.

#### 2. Step-labeled injection-hijack trajectory benchmark (AgentDrift)

AgentDrift replaces whole-trace guard judgments and attack-success-only benchmarks with a step-labeled corpus (12,536 trajectories, 71,024 steps labeled benign, injection point, hijacked, or failed injection) for training and evaluating detectors of indirect prompt injection in tool-call trajectories. Adoption costs the integration of an offline evaluation harness against one's own agent traces plus detector development, since the paper provides data and a weak baseline rather than a deployable guard model. Key failure risks include domain mismatch with the five covered domains, synthetic-data artifacts the authors themselves document (template concentration, attack-goal-family collapse, world-identity leakage, an LLM judge fooled by hard negatives), and reduced validity against adaptive attackers once the corpus is public.

#### 3. Database-style optimized context assembly pipeline

It replaces ad hoc prompt builders, scattered compaction routines, and per-provider cache workarounds with a unified five-phase pipeline (Plan, Bind, Optimize, Execute, Feedback) featuring a structured data-source catalog, a deterministic cache-aware optimizer, and an EXPLAIN ANALYZE trace for auditability and replay.
