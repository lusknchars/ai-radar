Extreme Compression of Per-Layer Embeddings via Cross-Layer Latent Vector Quantization

OPTIC replaces the conventional approach of storing an individual short code per token-layer vector (as in product quantization of per-layer embeddings) with a single quantized code tuple per token decoded at every layer through a layer-specific affine decoder fitted to the PLE output path. The cost is a fitting procedure against the PLE output path plus per-layer affine decoders, with negligible latency overhead (at most 0.40%) but a quality degradation that grows sharply beyond roughly 100x compression (90.5% at 594x, 84.1% at 1,002x). It can fail if the model in use does not have large per-layer embeddings comparable to Gemma 4 E4B's 35.2% parameter share, if the fitted codes transfer poorly across fine-tuned variants, or if the quality loss at higher compression rates is unacceptable for the target task.

Pruning for Efficiency, Paying in Fairness: Demographic Disparities in Pruned Speech-LLMs

The paper is an evaluation study, not a new compression method: it replaces the practice of selecting pruned speech-LLM checkpoints using aggregate WER alone with selection that also tracks per-demographic-group WER and the worst-performing group's error rate. The cost is additional evaluation compute (one inference pass per demographic slice per checkpoint) and extra analysis and dataset labeling, which is small relative to deployment risk. What can fail: the fairness effects are dataset-dependent (gaps widened on Fair-Speech but not clearly on Common Voice), so per-group metrics must be measured on the target population, and LoRA recovery after pruning can widen disparities even while improving every group's absolute WER.

RIDAR replaces manual or absent auditing of autonomous research-agent trajectories with a taxonomy-driven post-hoc detector (14 violation types) and a runtime monitor that intervenes mid-execution. PC-DAPS replaces single-trajectory Decoupled Annealing Posterior Sampling (DAPS) and best-of-k reruns for diffusion-based nonlinear inverse problems, maintaining multiple candidate solutions within one run and pruning them by prior consistency without ground-truth access. Repair Changes Which Layers to Remove: Exact Optima for Depth Pruning of Language Models

The method replaces heuristic, repair-agnostic layer-removal scores (as in SLEB and ReplaceMe) with a greedy search that selects layers under the linear repair that will actually be applied, plus a second-order loss score that identifies the repaired block from one forward and one backward pass of the intact model. Amplifying Latency and Cost of LLM Inference via Rowhammer Attack

LLVMhammer replaces nothing in the reader's stack; it is an attack that flips a DRAM bit in Triton's target-independent compilation pipeline so the compiler emits a functionally identical but slower GPU kernel, which then persists via Triton's on-disk kernel cache. The paper does not propose a new method to adopt; it replaces the implicit practices of leaving the chair agent's persona unspecified and of treating raw inter-run agreement as reliability, recommending instead that the chair persona be reported and systematically varied and that agreement be chance-corrected (e.g., Fleiss kappa).
