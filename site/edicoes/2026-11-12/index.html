Veritas: A Verifier Generation Harness for Agent Tasks

Veritas replaces hand-written task verifiers with automatically generated ones, using an evidence header, a domain-rule library, and authoring principles refined by a self-improvement loop. Six Layers Less: Encoder Pruning for Whisper with Label-Free Recovery

Replaces the full 32-layer Whisper large-v3-turbo encoder with a 26-layer encoder selected by leave-one-layer-out WER ranking, recovered via distillation on unlabeled monolingual speech. The cost is a mean WER increase of roughly 1.9 points (18.2% to 20.1%) plus a distillation run requiring unlabeled speech data and modest GPU time; inference itself needs no custom code. What can fail: the ~10% relative WER regression may be unacceptable for high-accuracy domains, the layer ranking and recovery may not transfer to languages or acoustic conditions outside the four evaluated, and realized latency gains depend on whether the encoder is the bottleneck in the deployment stack.

Deterministic, Reproducible Inference in Exact Integer Arithmetic, and Verifiable Distributed Inference as Its Consequence

The method replaces floating-point runtime kernels with an end-to-end exact integer arithmetic path (floating point confined to offline quantization), yielding bit-identical outputs across hardware, instruction sets, and distributed machines, verified via SHA-256 of outputs. It costs substantial storage (e.g., 427 GB of integer codes for a 106B MoE), offers no per-device speed-up (explicitly disclaimed), and confines fidelity to matching the float reference's decisions rather than bit-equal floating intermediates. Adoption risks include decision mismatches versus the float reference on untested inputs (numeric exceptions were measured, not eliminated), storage and memory footprints exceeding single-host RAM, and no guarantee against a dishonest server, which the authors defer to future work.

Dependable Long-Horizon LLM Agents: A Trajectory-Centered Empirical Study of Where Errors Enter and Which Controls Reach Them

This replaces symptom-level failure labeling (failure-mode tags, responsible-agent attribution) with a structured episode record that traces each fault's origin, propagation, detection, response, and consequence, and it motivates adopting specific runtime controls: independent evidence-reading judges, task contracts fixed at admission, and environment-grounded belief re-checks. It replaces unbounded admission and simple throttling in prefix-cached agent serving (vLLM/SGLang) with a budgeter that caps the resident KV demand of active sessions to the KV pool, suspending admitted sessions when over capacity. Looping Deeper Without Growing the KV Cache: Recurrent Depth for Causal Encoder-Decoders

The method replaces per-layer and per-round KV caching in looped language models with an asymmetric causal encoder-decoder in which a recurrently looped encoder refines a single globally shared KV cache via stochastic-depth training to a fixed point, eliminating round-wise cache growth. Parallel Broyden Methods for Efficiently Evaluating Nonlinear State Space Models

Replaces sequential evaluation of nonlinear state space models (and exact parallel Newton with dense Jacobian multiplies) with a parallel Broyden quasi-Newton method using diagonal-plus-low-rank Jacobian approximations built from trajectory secants, avoiding automatic differentiation. Bits or permutations? Spending a fixed inference budget on weight precision or on option-order averaging in small local language models

The method replaces fp16 weight storage and single-order multiple-choice evaluation with 6-bit round-to-nearest quantized weights plus averaging over up to four cyclic permutations of answer options. The cost is a multiplicative inference pass increase (up to 4x forward passes) traded against roughly 2.7x memory reduction from 6-bit weights, priced jointly as weight-bytes-read times passes. What can fail: the findings rest on two small models, a simulated quantizer, 300 items per benchmark, and an inconclusive noise control on slot skew, so the +1.8-point anomaly at 6 bits and the cost-matched decision table may not transfer to other models, real quantization kernels, or larger evaluation sets.

SkipLane: Write-Once, Conflict-Free Accumulation for Zero-Skipping CNN Engines on FPGAs

SkipLane replaces read-modify-write row-buffer accumulation in zero-skipping CNN engines with write-once per-kernel-row lanes that retire finished columns into conflict-free memory banks, summing lanes only at readout. It costs additional FPGA logic (three registers per lane, multiple banks, 21.1k LUTs for six engines) and requires a custom RTL accelerator design rather than commodity hardware. It can fail on layers with short output rows: conv2 reaches only 0.87x dense speed, and all results are RTL/board results on LeNet-5, so gains may not generalize to larger networks.

Emergent Biological Capabilities in a Foundation Model for Molecular Interactions

Bonbon replaces structure-dependent molecular interaction pipelines (co-folding, docking-based binding-site and affinity prediction, and task-specific supervised models) with zero-shot inference from a frozen protein-ligand sequence foundation model. The cost is dependence on a 1.6B-parameter pretrained model the reader cannot train themselves, plus validation overhead to confirm emergent capabilities transfer to their specific targets; inference itself is lightweight and API-compatible. It can fail on molecular series far from the training distribution, emergent capabilities may not transfer to novel protein families, and the model offers no structural coordinates when atom-level pose information is required.

Veritas: A Verifier Generation Harness for Agent Tasks

agent evaluation · test · 4 of 5 implementations are independent

Veritas replaces hand-written task verifiers with automatically generated ones, using an evidence header, a domain-rule library, and authoring principles refined by a self-improvement loop. The cost is additional LLM calls for verifier generation plus maintaining the rule library and generation pipeline, and gains are measured as agreement with official verdicts rather than end-task quality. It can fail where generated verifiers encode incorrect or incomplete domain rules, where agreement with official benchmarks does not transfer to the reader's own tasks, or where self-improvement refinement drifts without ground-truth checks.
