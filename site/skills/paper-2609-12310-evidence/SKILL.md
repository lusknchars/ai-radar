## Reported claims

- The method replaces a full 20B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a layer-wise pruned specialist, ranking experts by task-specific routing mass and allocating retained capacity via cross-lingual routing divergence, followed by recovery fine-tuning on synthetic data and MXFP4 quantization of the remaining expert weights. It costs a multi-stage pipeline: routing-statistics collection over task data, synthetic recovery-tuning data generated by a proprietary frontier model (GPT-5.1), retraining compute, and a custom inference stack with category inference, validation, retries, and fallback logic. It can fail when routing statistics do not transfer to unseen domains, when pruned experts carry rare-but-critical capability, when MXFP4 quantization compounds pruning error, or when the synthetic teacher data biases the specialist toward teacher artifacts, and the reported quality rests on ps (inferred)
- Experts are physically removed from GPT-OSS-20B to reach 4.186B-7.770B total parameters (roughly 2.6x-4.8x parameter reduction) with packed artifacts of 4.55-6.33 GiB; quality is supported only by internal xCOMET-XL scoring against GPT-5.1 pseudo-references, not an independent benchmark. (inferred)
