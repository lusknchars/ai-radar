## Reported claims

- The paper is a governance review, not an engineering method: it proposes replacing ad-hoc or absent biosecurity controls with tiered, capability-threshold-linked responsibilities across the biological AI stack, rather than replacing any specific model component. Adopting its framework costs organizational effort—capability evaluations, screening-evasion benchmarks, and audit processes—rather than GPU memory or latency, though any applied safeguards can add refusal overhead and false-positive blocking of legitimate queries. It can fail because alignment techniques transfer poorly to biological foundation models, so hazardous capabilities may persist undetected despite applied mitigations, and current uplift evidence is largely in-silico, meaning thresholds may be miscalibrated.
