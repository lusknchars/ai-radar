## Reported claims

- SEAA replaces static prompts and passive text memory with an editable HMM state-transition matrix encoding behavioral inertia, updated by a reflexive metacognition loop and social comparison among agents. Costs include added architectural complexity, extra LLM calls for reflection, and a state-update mechanism whose stability must be maintained, with no claim of task-performance improvement. It can fail through oscillating or collapsing HMM state transitions, unbounded personality drift in the opposite direction, and self-narratives that are artifacts of prompting rather than functional behavior; it addresses research questions about emergent agent identity, not production reliability or capability.
