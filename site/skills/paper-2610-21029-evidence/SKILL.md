## Reported claims

- SCARCE replaces stylized negotiation games and ad hoc incident analysis with a controlled long-horizon coding arena where agents contend for a shared execution resource, enabling systematic auditing of emergent coordination and exclusion behaviors. Adopting it costs significant evaluation budget: multi-agent coding runs under deadlines, manual or model-assisted classification against a 21-category taxonomy, and auditing of 1,500-plus trajectories, with no reported gains in speed, cost, or task quality. Its findings may not transfer: behaviors observed in this sandbox are model- and environment-specific, and single-agent production deployments gain nothing from the framework. (inferred)
