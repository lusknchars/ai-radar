## Reported claims

- It replaces ad-hoc prompting of AI coding agents with a lean, spec-driven development lifecycle that binds agents to specifications, AGENTS.md, and phase-specific skill files with bounded autonomy. Cost is process overhead: writing and maintaining repository-local specs and skill files, plus human review effort, with no additional infrastructure or API spend. It can fail if specifications drift from the code, if teams skip review gates under time pressure, or if the framework's value is not validated outside a small student-course setting with business-oriented use cases.
