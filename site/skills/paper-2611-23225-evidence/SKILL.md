## Reported claims

- StringQFT replaces ad hoc sourcing of string-theory and QFT reference material with a curated 18-volume textbook collection plus a local lexical retriever, benchmark items, and 19 finite-domain calculation tools for AI-assisted theoretical physics. It costs the effort of hosting or indexing roughly 3,795 pages locally and wiring the retriever and tools into an agent workflow, with no foundation-model training required. It can fail through lexical-retrieval misses on mathematical notation, tool outputs that are correct only within their specified finite domains, and limited transfer to general ML engineering work.
