## Reported claims

- This approach replaces static LLM-as-a-Judge systems that rely solely on fixed execution logs by introducing active evaluators that autonomously seek external evidence and verify environmental states. It significantly increases operational costs through multi-step agentic inference, tool-use API fees, and orchestration complexity compared to passive, single-pass evaluation methods. The system can fail due to unreliable tool calls, compounding reasoning errors, or when the judge's own agentic capabilities are insufficient to retrieve or interpret the necessary evidence.
