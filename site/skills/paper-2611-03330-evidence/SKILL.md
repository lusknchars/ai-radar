## Reported claims

- The SIMPLE law replaces per-model empirical fitting with a closed-form analytical model to predict inference energy from architectural hyperparameters. It costs engineering effort to implement the formula and integrate it into capacity planning or scheduling systems, rather than requiring runtime calibration per LLM. The model can fail when applied to hardware or architectures outside its validated set (13 dense LLMs on H100) or when workload characteristics deviate from the traces used for validation.
