## Reported claims

- LUDIUS replaces ad hoc or absent evaluation of vision-language-model social judgment in embodied agents with a structured benchmark of 15 social intelligence skills tested across simulated week-long household episodes, including a harness that feeds camera views, retrieves interaction history, and converts tool calls into robot actions. It costs nothing directly to the reader but adopting its methodology requires simulation environments, episode generation, and finetuning data that are not available under a constrained infrastructure budget. Its findings indicate that frontier models fail when reasoning about what a resident perceived earlier, reconciling competing interests, or acting under safety constraints and household norms, so any agent evaluated only on simpler tasks may overestimate its readiness for socially constrained deployment. (inferred)
