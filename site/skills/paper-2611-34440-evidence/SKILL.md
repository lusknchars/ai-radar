## Reported claims

- vSFT replaces in-context verbal self-correction (e.g., Reflexion) and on-policy agentic RL (e.g., GRPO) with test-time low-rank weight updates generated by a hypernetwork from supervisor feedback on failed trajectories. The cost is a closed-loop pipeline requiring a supervisor model, a trained hypernetwork, and per-episode weight updates, adding serving complexity and latency beyond plain prompting, plus the offline meta-training of the hypernetwork itself. It can fail if the supervisor's verbal feedback is noisy or unfaithful to the failure cause, if low-rank updates interfere destructively across episodes (catastrophic drift), or if gains demonstrated on ALFWorld and WebShop do not transfer to the reader's domain. (inferred)
- vSFT on Qwen3-4B outperforms GRPO and Reflexion by 8.7% and 13.9% success rate on ALFWorld and by 4.8% and 18.2% on WebShop (inferred)
