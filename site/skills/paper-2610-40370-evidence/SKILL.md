## Reported claims

- It replaces passive LLM health recommendation with a registry-based loop in which mechanistic physiological models make pre-registered probabilistic predictions, beliefs update only from logged fits, and a planner prescribes constrained informative training. The cost is substantial domain modeling, wearable-data infrastructure, calibration effort, and safety-review overhead rather than GPU training. It can fail through model misspecification, weak segment-time forecasting, biased adaptation as the athlete changes, and overconstraint by safety or evidence gates. (inferred)
