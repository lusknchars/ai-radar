## Reported claims

- The method replaces answer-level benchmark scoring with statement-level provenance receipts, condition-aware scoring, and human-adversarial verification when evaluating agentic QA systems. It costs additional evaluation engineering: extracting claims, attaching source evidence per claim, designing buried-evidence test conditions, and allocating human review time, all feasible on a single GPU or API budget. It can fail if receipts are generated by the same model being audited, if claim extraction misses fabricated structural assertions attached to accurate numbers, or if the human verification step becomes a rubber stamp under production pressure.
