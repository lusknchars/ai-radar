## Reported claims

- This survey introduces PAPAV, a taxonomy that replaces architecture-specific comparisons of computer-use and robot-use agents with five functional capabilities (Perceive, Anticipate, Plan, Act, Verify), so it is an analytical lens rather than a deployable method. It costs nothing to run but offers no model, code, or benchmark improvement; its value is limited to framing evaluation and design. Its survey finding that only 5 of 64 benchmarks assess Anticipate and 3 assess Verify indicates current agent evaluations over-weight task success, but adopting the framework cannot itself close those gaps and may not map cleanly onto an engineer's existing evaluation harness.
