## Reported claims

- The method replaces single critical-mass thresholds for adversarial takeover of multi-agent systems with a directed, weighted map of transitions between coordination equilibria, including indirect tipping through intermediate states. It costs analytic and experimental effort to model agent population dynamics rather than compute or model capacity, and it yields threat-modeling insight rather than a deployable defense component. It can fail when its population-level abstraction does not match the actual interaction graph, agent heterogeneity, or communication channels of a production deployment, leaving the mapped thresholds unvalidated for that system.
