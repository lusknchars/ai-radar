Stochastic Estimation of Transduced Language Models

Replaces threshold pruning with beam summation, which yields a lower bound with unknown error, with reweighted sampling without replacement for unbiased prefix-probability estimates. It requires recursive sampling with inclusion probabilities and a transduced language-model architecture outside typical LLM API or open-model stacks. Its applications are computational psycholinguistics, bioinformatics, and formal language theory rather than everyday ML engineering on small GPUs.
