Multimodal Alignment Through Joint Kernel Entropic Gromov--Wasserstein Optimal Transport

Replaces labeled-pair cross-modal fine-tuning with post-hoc alignment of pretrained encoder embeddings using quadratic optimal transport. A low-rank kernel approximation and alternating variational solver add implementation and computational cost, although working on embeddings allows a CPU or small GPU. Weak unimodal encoders or incompatible within-modality similarity structures can undermine the method, which preserves geometry already present in the embeddings.
