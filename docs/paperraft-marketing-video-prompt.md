# Paperraft product video prompt

Create a 30-second product video for Paperraft, an AI research reading tool for developers. Use actual screen recordings of the current application as the product footage. Keep interface text and paper titles intact. Deliver 1920×1080 at 30 fps with an English voiceover and readable captions. Keep key content centered for a later vertical edit.

Art direction: quiet, precise, editorial. Paper gray #EEEEEE, ink #202024, raspberry #CB2957, white #FFFFFF. Use the existing Paperraft typography. Let paper sheets and a single highlighted passage suggest attention. Use restrained cursor movement, page transitions and soft clicks. Background music should stay below the voiceover.

0–5 seconds: Open on a real paper PDF, then pull back to the Paperraft archive. On-screen text: “Which AI papers deserve your time?” Voiceover: “Another promising AI paper. Is it relevant to what you're building?”

5–11 seconds: Search the archive and open a paper brief. Frame the title and explanation of the technical change. Voiceover: “Paperraft gives you a reading guide to the method and its reported findings.”

11–17 seconds: Click through the actual paper-page stack. Open the source PDF at the selected page. Voiceover: “Preview the original pages, then follow the source.”

17–23 seconds: Show the exposure map for paper 2608.21223. Open one source excerpt. Keep the distinction between source-linked findings, interpretations and unknowns legible. Voiceover: “See what the authors report, what is inferred, and what still needs checking.”

23–27 seconds: Click “Download research skill” and show the resulting ZIP contents, SKILL.md and evidence.json. Voiceover: “Download a research skill to carry that context into your agent.”

27–30 seconds: End on the Paperraft wordmark with a subtle ASCII ripple behind it. On-screen text: “Read the evidence. Choose your next experiment.” CTA: “Explore Paperraft · paperaft.vercel.app”. Voiceover: “Paperraft. Choose your next experiment.”

Use genuine recordings for every interaction. Capture the preview/download scenes from a build containing this feature. Do not fabricate benchmark gains, testimonials, user counts or successful reproductions. Do not imply that all papers have full-text analysis, that downloaded skills implement the techniques, or that Paperraft has run GPU experiments. Do not animate invented UI screens or replace the scientific content with decorative text.
