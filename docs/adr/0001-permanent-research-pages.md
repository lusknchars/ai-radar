# Permanent research pages precede deep reports

Every indexed paper receives a stable `/papers/<arxiv-id>/` research page, while
`/reports/<arxiv-id>/` remains an optional full-text analysis. The research page
keeps the same URL as its editorial status matures, publishes the structured
model as JSON, and renders missing analysis as "not evaluated." This costs more
generated files than linking directly to arXiv, but it gives readers a durable
address and prevents an absent deep report from looking like an absent risk.
